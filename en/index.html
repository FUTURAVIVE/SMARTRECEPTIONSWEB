How it works

From invitation to entry, in six steps

From the list to access, in six steps

The host creates the visit

One by one or in bulk, with configurable time slots and margins.

The visitor receives their QR

By email or WhatsApp, with the appointment details.

The system validates on arrival

It checks the invitation and time slot in seconds.

Access opens

The turnstile or door can open automatically.

The visit is logged

Full, exportable access traceability.

If there's an incident, reception steps in

Reception or security resolve it in one click.

Attendees are imported or synced

From CSV, Excel, Eventbrite or FareHarbor.

Each guest receives a QR

By email or WhatsApp, ready for the day of the event.

Staff scan with their smartphone

A simplified interface built for the door.

The screen shows valid or rejected

A clear, immediate decision for the staff.

The QR is consumed after access

One single access per code, no duplicates.

Incidents are escalated to a Manager

Separate roles for Staff, Manager and Administrator.

Control and trust

  • Reception and security can step in at any moment.
